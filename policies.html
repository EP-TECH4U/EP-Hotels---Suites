Privacy Policy

EP Hotels & Suites is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide during booking, check-in, and through our website, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and postal address
  • Payment details and billing information
  • Government-issued ID for legal compliance
  • Preferences, special requests, and stay history
  • Website usage data via cookies and analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to:

  • Process reservations and payments
  • Provide personalized guest services
  • Communicate booking confirmations and updates
  • Improve our services and website experience
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Data Security & Sharing

We use SSL encryption and secure servers to protect your data. We do not sell your information. Data may be shared only with trusted partners for payment processing, legal compliance, or with your consent.

4. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting privacy@ephotels.com. You may also opt out of marketing communications at any time.

Cancellation Policy

We understand plans change. Please review our cancellation terms below.

Rate Type Cancellation Window Penalty
Flexible Rate Free cancellation until 6:00 PM, 1 day before arrival No charge
Advance Purchase Non-refundable, non-changeable 100% charge at booking
Group Bookings 5+ rooms Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival 50% charge within 14 days
No-Show Guest does not arrive and did not cancel 1 night charge + taxes

Early Departure

Guests checking out before the reserved departure date will be charged for the full reserved stay unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Force Majeure

In case of natural disasters, government restrictions, or other unforeseen events, we offer free rebooking within 12 months or a full refund at management discretion.

Terms of Service

1. Reservations & Check-In

Check-in time is 3:00 PM. Check-out is 12:00 PM. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and may incur fees. A valid government ID and credit card are required at check-in.

2. Guest Conduct

Guests must respect hotel property and other guests. Smoking is prohibited in all rooms and incurs a $250 cleaning fee. Parties and excessive noise are not permitted. Management reserves the right to evict guests violating policies without refund.

3. Liability

EP Hotels & Suites is not responsible for loss or damage to personal property. Safe deposit boxes are available. Guests are liable for any damage to hotel property caused by them or their visitors.

4. Children & Pets

Children under 12 stay free using existing bedding. Cribs available on request. Pets are not permitted except certified service animals.

5. Website Use

All content on this website is property of EP Hotels & Suites. You may not reproduce or distribute content without permission. Rates and availability are subject to change.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time is check-in and check-out? +
Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is by 12:00 PM. Early check-in or late check-out may be arranged based on availability. A fee may apply for late check-out after 2:00 PM.
Do you offer airport pickup? +
Yes. We offer complimentary luxury SUV airport transfers for all direct bookings. Please provide your flight details at least 24 hours before arrival to schedule pickup.
Is breakfast included in the room rate? +
Breakfast inclusion depends on your rate. Most Flexible and Executive rates include daily buffet breakfast at Café Lumière. Advance Purchase rates are room-only unless specified.
What is your pet policy? +
We do not allow pets, with the exception of certified service animals as defined by law. Documentation may be required at check-in.
Do you have parking? +
Yes. Complimentary valet parking is available for all hotel guests. Self-parking is also available in our secured underground garage at no charge.
How can I modify or cancel my reservation? +
You can manage your booking through the link in your confirmation email, or contact our reservations team at +234 800 EP HOTEL or reservations@ephotels.com. Cancellation terms depend on your rate type.
